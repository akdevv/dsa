## Daily Approach

1. Brute force first, always.
2. Optimize after you have something running.
3. Struggle 20–40 min. Peek hint only after 15–20 min of zero progress.
4. Re-solve anything you peeked, cold, next day.
5. One-line pattern note in your own words.

---

## Rules

**Never miss twice.** One skip is noise. Two in a row is how this dies. Dead day? Do 1 problem, 15 min. Keep the chain alive.

**Peeked ≠ learned.** If you looked at the solution, re-solve it cold or it doesn't count. This rule alone is the gap between "I did 474" and "I can do 474."

**Weekends = retention.** Re-solve 2–3 old problems cold. Otherwise you reach DP having forgotten binary search.

## When You Want to Quit

You will want to quit around week 4–5. The easy topics are gone, the grind feels endless, and your brain will offer a very reasonable story about why "starting fresh next month" is smarter.

It's not. The deadline exists _specifically_ to beat that voice. You don't need motivation — you need to do the next 8 problems. That's the whole job.

Two months from now you're either interview-ready or telling yourself the same story again. You already picked — it's written at the top of this file.
